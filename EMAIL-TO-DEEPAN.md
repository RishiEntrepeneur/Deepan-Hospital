**To:** [Dr. Deepan / hospital administrator]
**Subject:** Deepan Hospital booking website — ready to launch, one item needed from Klinique

Dear [Dr. Deepan],

The patient booking website for Deepan Hospital is built and ready to go live.
Patients can find a doctor, book an appointment, and pay — in English, Tamil
and Hindi, on a phone or a computer.

Everything on our side is working today:

- Patients book appointments online, at any hour.
- Your reception desk sees every booking the moment it arrives, and approves it.
- Reception files each booking into Klinique — either from a simple on-screen
  list, or with a one-click browser tool I have provided that fills the Klinique
  form for them.
- No booking is ever lost.

There is one improvement that needs your help to switch on: making bookings
appear in Klinique **fully automatically**, with no reception step at all. This
is the one thing the website cannot do on its own, because Klinique — quite
correctly — sits behind a login. It needs something only the hospital or
Klinique can provide.

Any **one** of the following would enable it, in my order of preference:

1. **A public patient self-booking link or widget from Klinique.** Klinique's
   platform advertises patient self-booking. If your subscription includes — or
   can be upgraded to include — a patient-facing booking page we can link to or
   embed, that is the best option: patients would book straight into your
   Klinique schedule with nothing in between. Please ask Klinique whether this
   is available for your account.

2. **API access from Klinique.** If Klinique can provide an appointment-booking
   API — its documentation and an access key — the website will post every
   booking directly and automatically.

3. **A dedicated Klinique login for the website.** If neither of the above is
   available, please create (or ask Klinique to create) a staff login in
   Klinique named something like "Website Bookings", and share the username and
   password with me. The website will use it to file bookings by itself. It
   should be a separate account, not a person's, so it is clearly identified and
   can be switched off at any time.

To move this forward, could you either:

- put me in touch with your Klinique account manager or support contact, or
- forward the short note below to Klinique on my behalf.

I am happy to speak to Klinique directly and handle all the technical details —
I just need the introduction, or one of the three items above.

Until any of these is in place, the website is fully usable and can go live now;
reception simply files bookings into Klinique as I have shown them. The
automatic option can be turned on the day we have one of the three.

Thank you,
