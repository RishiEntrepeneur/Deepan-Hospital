A short guide for doctors — the new online appointment booking

Patients can now book appointments on the hospital's website. This guide explains what that means for you. The short version: nothing changes in how you work. Every online booking arrives in Klinique — where you already see your patients — exactly like a booking made at the front desk.

How a booking reaches you

1. Patientbooks on the website (phone or computer)
→
2. Receptionapproves it and enters it into Klinique
→
3. Yousee it in your Klinique appointment list, as always

You do not need to check the website or learn anything new. Your queue in Klinique is still the one place your appointments appear.

If you want to see the booking desk yourself

You have a login to the hospital's booking desk — useful if you want to watch appointments arrive, or check your own schedule is open for booking. It is optional.

Your usernamedoctor followed by your name — e.g. doctordeepan, doctorpriyanka
Your passwordAsk the administrator to set one for you (they run one command and read it to you). Change of password is on request.
Where to sign inThe hospital website → Sign in → I work at the hospital
Once signed in you land on the reception desk, and the Klinique tab opens the full Klinique portal — including your own physician login — without leaving the page.

Making sure patients can book you

A patient can only book you online once your consulting days, session times and fee are set. Reception does this from the desk (Schedules). If you are not appearing as bookable on the website, that is the thing to check — ask reception to open your schedule.

What patients see about you

  • Your name, speciality and qualifications.
  • Your registration number — only if the hospital has entered it. It is never invented.
  • Your consulting times and consultation fee.
  • No personal contact details. Patients reach you through the hospital, not directly.
In short: patients book online → reception puts it in Klinique → it appears in your list. Your day does not change. Your login to the desk is there if you want it, but you do not need it to see your appointments.
