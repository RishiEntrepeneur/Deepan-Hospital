Booking, online and in three languages.

A public booking page for Deepan Hospital and a reception screen behind it. It sits in front of Klinique — it does not replace it, and it does not touch the clinical record. This document describes what has been built, what is still needed, and what happens next.

The problem it solves

Today a patient who wants an appointment at Deepan Hospital has to telephone, during working hours, and speak to somebody at the counter. There is no other way in. Someone deciding at eleven at night, or who would rather read Tamil than speak English to a stranger, or who simply cannot get through, goes to whichever hospital they can reach first.

Klinique holds the hospital's clinical record and does that job well, but it has no public page — a patient cannot reach it. That gap is what this fills.

For the patient

Books in about a minute, with no account

Choose a department or a doctor, see the real free times, book. No app to download and nothing to sign up for — an account is offered, never required.

Every screen in English, Tamil and Hindi

Not a translated front page with English underneath: the whole site, including the booking form, the fees, the departments and the appointment slip.

Sees the fee before booking

Each consultant's fee, days and consulting hours are shown as the hospital records them, so nobody arrives expecting a different number.

Gets the appointment in writing

Reference, doctor, date, time and the hospital's address, sent by WhatsApp or SMS in the patient's own language.

For reception

Bookings arrive on their screen by themselves

With a chime and an on-screen alert. Nobody types anything, and no booking waits for somebody to check an inbox.

The slot is held the moment a patient books

Two patients cannot take the same time. The database enforces it, not the screen — so it holds even if two people book in the same second.

Booking for a caller is faster than the register

The free times for any doctor on any date, one tap to hold one, and a reference to read back to the caller.

A worklist for Klinique, with a tick

Every booking to be entered, each field one tap to copy, and a record of which have been done — so none is entered twice or missed.

It does not compete with Klinique

There is deliberately no second place to write a prescription or a consultation note. Two records that can disagree about one patient is a real danger, and avoiding it was worth more than the convenience. Klinique stays the clinical record; this is the booking page in front of it.

On accreditation and registration numbers

The site makes no NABH claim, and every consultant's registration field has been left empty rather than filled in with a plausible guess. Both are the hospital's to confirm from its own records. An unverified claim on a hospital's public site is the kind of thing that is cheap to avoid now and expensive to explain later.

What is needed from the hospital

Five things, none of which can be supplied from outside. The first two are what stand between today and going live.

  1. A domain name and hosting

    Registered in the hospital's name, not a supplier's. Patient data and the hospital's own address should never sit in somebody else's account.

  2. Medical registration numbers for all 18 consultants

    From the hospital's records. These appear publicly beside each doctor's qualification.

  3. Confirmation of the consulting hours and fees

    They were transcribed from the hospital's own OP list. Somebody should read them once before patients start booking against them.

  4. A decision on who texts the patient

    Klinique's booking form can already send an SMS confirmation. If the hospital would rather it did so, that is the simpler route and reception need do nothing.

  5. A named person for data questions

    Required under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 — a patient must have somebody to write to. A name and an email address is enough.

Next steps

Two phases. The first is short, because most of the work is done.

Phase one — go live

Domain, secure hosting, off-site backups, the registration numbers entered, hours and fees confirmed, and reception trained on the manual. The site is then open to patients.

Phase two — remove the typing

Bookings pass into Klinique automatically instead of being entered by hand. Needs either an API key from Klinique or one booking captured from their own form. Quoted separately, because it depends on what Klinique will provide.

Suggested first step

Bring two or three departments live for a fortnight rather than the whole roster at once. Reception learns the screen on a small volume, the hospital sees real bookings before committing further, and the remaining departments are opened by ticking a box.
